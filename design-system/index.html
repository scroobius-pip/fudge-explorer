Questions

What visitors actually ask

What does Fudge check?

A real captured website or screenshot: layout, typography, color, and related visual references. It does not invent a design from a blank prompt.

What do I receive?

A readable answer with the same reference identity: title, source, screenshot, and the details that matter for the task. You can open that reference in Explorer, save it, or hand it to another AI tool.

Can I use a screenshot instead of a URL?

Yes. Paste a URL, describe the job, or attach a screenshot in Chat. Fudge routes that intent without turning Explorer, Chat, and MCP into separate products.

Does one paid plan include Explorer, Chat, and hosted MCP?

Yes. Pro and Studio are the same Fudge product at different capacity. Explorer, Chat, saved references, prompt handoff, and hosted MCP are included.

Can I use Fudge from Cursor or Claude?

Yes. Hosted MCP is the same capability from your AI tool. Setup appears when you choose to work that way; it is not a top-level product.

Does a reference stay the same if I ask Fudge about it?

Yes. Title, source, and screenshot stay attached when you inspect, save, discuss, or hand the reference off.
